# RAR invite-only beta kit

## Scope

Run one cohort of 10–20 manga collectors. This document prepares the test; it does not authorise invitations, messages, or access changes.

RAR is a catalogue and collection tracker with evidence-backed market context. It is not investment advice, and an unpriced holding is still a valid collection record.

## Invite copy

RAR is testing a private beta for manga collectors. Please use a non-critical account, add five manga you already own, and share your shelf only if you are comfortable making those edition identities public. Public shelves never show purchase price, purchase date, notes, quantity, email, or account ID.

Please do not enter a sale unless you have the working original link to a completed sale. Active listings and ended-unsold listings are buying leads, not price evidence.

## Collector task

1. Find one edition by title and one by ISBN.
2. Add five holdings, including quantity and cost only when you want to track them.
3. Edit one holding and remove one test holding.
4. Explain, in your own words, the difference between a latest sale, an evidence-backed value, an active listing, and “not enough evidence”.
5. Claim a collector handle. Review the shelf privacy explanation, publish the shelf only if comfortable, open the shared URL in a signed-out browser, then make it private again.
6. Request one genuinely missing edition, or say that you could not find one.
7. Return the feedback template below.

## Feedback template

- Device and browser:
- Could you find the exact edition? **Yes / No**
- Could you add five holdings without help? **Yes / No**
- Did totals and unpriced items make sense? **Yes / No / Not shown**
- Could you tell completed sales from active listings? **Yes / No**
- Did the shelf preview explain what becomes public? **Yes / No**
- Shared shelf URL tested signed out: **Yes / No / Chose not to publish**
- One wrong fact, broken action, or confusing sentence:
- One missing manga you expected:
- Minutes to finish:
- Support messages needed:
- Would you return next week to update the shelf? **Yes / No / Maybe**

For catalogue or sale corrections, include the exact RAR page and the original publisher, library, or completed-sale URL. A report remains a review lead until a human verifies it.

## Cohort metrics

Record only aggregate counts unless a tester explicitly agrees to be quoted.

| Metric | How to count |
| --- | --- |
| Start rate | Invitees who sign in and begin ÷ invitations accepted |
| Five-holding completion | Testers who save five holdings ÷ testers who begin |
| Exact-edition success | Testers who find both requested editions without help ÷ testers who begin |
| Value comprehension | Testers who correctly distinguish value/latest sale/live listing/insufficient evidence ÷ completed responses |
| Share success | Testers who choose to publish and can open the URL signed out ÷ testers who try publishing |
| Missing-edition success | Requests saved with a usable source ÷ requests attempted |
| Return rate | Testers who return within 7 days ÷ testers who complete |
| Support burden | Total owner support messages and minutes ÷ testers who begin |
| Critical defects | Privacy leaks, cross-account writes, incorrect edition attachment, invented/unsourced sales, or valuation groups being combined |

## Release gate

Do not widen the beta while any critical defect is open. Before the next cohort:

- every participating tester can keep a shelf private;
- a published shelf has been checked signed out and exposes only the documented fields;
- add, edit, delete, quantity, and optional cost have completed successfully on mobile and desktop;
- unpriced holdings remain usable and are not shown as zero-value holdings;
- no tester mistakes an active listing for valuation evidence;
- all high-severity defects have an owner and a retest result;
- support burden is small enough that the owner can support the next cohort.

Actual recruitment, invitations, tester support, and collection of feedback are owner actions.
