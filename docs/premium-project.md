# RAR Premium Project

## First phase (preview, no billing)

- The free collector shelf remains public at the owner's choice and still includes up to 16 displayed books, title, caption, bio, book notes, likes, and comments.
- Preview access is granted manually in `collector_premium_access`; this is not a payment or subscription record. The initial founder preview is for `@shamar`.
- Preview collectors can choose Studio (the original look), Saffron, or Indigo for their profile and 3D gallery. The visitor's saved Day/Night preference is not overwritten; each finish has both appearances.
- A collector can opt into a text-only Premium badge. It is hidden by default and never denotes verified ownership, books, covers, or sales. The public page and directory display it only when both access and the owner's choice are present.
- The database trigger rejects custom themes and badges from accounts without preview access, even if they bypass the editor.

## Later phases, not yet built

1. Guided collector tour: the owner chooses an order and short introductions; visitors can still browse freely.
2. Guest postcards: visitors choose a few public books and share a card linking back to the collector's public gallery.
3. Owner-uploaded photos with notes: Premium collectors can show their own copy photos, visible to everyone, clearly labelled as owner photos and kept separate from verified catalogue covers. No blurred-photo paywall.
4. Billing and pricing only after the experience and moderation/storage costs are tested. Do not enable charges as part of the preview.
